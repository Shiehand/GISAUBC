Secrets to Clubs, Societies, and Getting Involved at UBC

UBC is no stranger to diverse communities and large lecture halls. Although some classes are smaller in size, it can still be difficult to explore your non-academic interests in the classroom. However, there is truly something for every UBC student. One of the best ways to find your niche on campus is by pushing away that social fear and get yourself involved!

Below are some ways that you can establish long-lasting relationships or build your community in UBC

CLUBS

Joining a student club is one of the best ways to discover your interest outside of your academics. From quidditch (yes, the one in Harry Potter) to Acapella, feel free to explore what UBC has to offer on Campus Base. At the moment, UBC has over 300 AMS clubs. With such a wide variety of clubs offered at UBC, you will receive the opportunity to engage with like-minded individuals outside of your academic cohort.

Below is a list of how to connect with all of these clubs:

  1. Attend Imagine UBC (Early September)
    Imagine UBC replaces the first day of classes and is a terrific way to meet your community. Campus clubs will be holding informative booths (held virtually this year) to accommodate your questions and host some fun games... I would encourage you to attend so you can get an idea of the club’s vibes.




  2. AMS Clubs Days (Late September)
    Explore more club booths in late September! (keep an eye for any updates on the AMS website). Most clubs will open their booth again and it is a perfect chance for you to ask about memberships or any open hiring!




  3. Contact Club Execs or Social Media
    All clubs remain open throughout the school year so don’t shy away from shooting that one club a DM on Instagram! Don’t feel beaten down if they leave you on reading, it’s probably unintentional. Send them a follow-up instead.

Student Societies

All of the faculties at UBC are represented by a student-faculty. You can discover faculty-specific events held by your faculty’s society. The best way to connect to them is through their official website, Instagram or Facebook. Societies are a terrific way for you to encounter like-minded individuals interested in a similar discourse. I have listed below an extensive list on the societies’ websites:

  • Arts Undergraduate Society (AUS)
  • Commerce Undergraduate Society (CUS)
  • Engineering Undergraduate Society (EUS)
  • Forestry Undergraduate Society (FUS)
  • Kinesiology Undergraduate Society (KUS)
  • Land and Food Systems Undergraduate Society (LFSUS)
  • Science Undergraduate Society (SUS)
  • UBC Vancouver School of Economics Undergraduate Society (VSEUS)

Greek Life

If you are interested in joining a sorority or a fraternity, Greek life may be for you. Aside from their fun social events and their occasional parties, you can participate in philanthropic endeavors and multiple leadership opportunities.

More Ways to Get Involved

  • Write for The Ubyssey or Discorder Magazine
  • Fix bicycles at the Bike Kitchen
  • Run intramural teams with the UBC Intramurals Student Staff team or apply for a position at UBC Recreation
  • Google “AMS Volunteering” or “UBC Clubs” and it should direct you to Campus Base for an exhaustive list of available opportunities!

UBC has a place for everyone regardless of their background, interest, and race. Join them and expand your network! You might be able to discover some hidden talents or new skills. I will now flip the question back to you: how are you thinking of getting involved?
