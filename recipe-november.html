Klepon

Preparation time: 20 minutes
Cooking time: 10 minutes
Servings: 21 balls
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Chewy and sweet, easy to make and will definitely remind you of home!
  • 125g of glutinous rice flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 150mL of boiling water
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 200g of shredded coconut
  • 100g of palm sugar
  • 4 drops of pandan flavouring
  • 125g tepung tapioka
  • 1 sdm gula
  • 1 jumput garam
  • 150mL air mendidih
  • 1 sdm minyak goreng
  • 200g kelapa parut
  • 100g gula merah
  • 4 tetes perisa pandan
  1. Cut palm sugar into smaller pieces.
  2. Mix glutinous rice flour with sugar and salt.
  3. Add in water little by little until a dough is formed and not sticky.
  4. Add in 4 drops of pandan flavoring, mix well.
  5. Weigh out around 20-25g of dough and roll into balls.
  6. Press down the balls and add in cut out palm sugar in the middle, roll it back into cubes.
  7. Boil water and add in the glutinous balls, boil until the balls float.
  8. Coat the balls with shredded coconut and serve!
  9. Best eaten after 30 mins!
  1. Potong gula merah menjadi potongan kecil.
  2. Campurkan tepung tapioka dengan gula dan garam.
  3. Tambahkan air panas secara bertahap hingga terbentuk adonan dan tidak lagi lengket.
  4. Tambahkan perisa pandan ke dalam adonan.
  5. Timbang sekitar 20-25g adonan dan bulatkan.
  6. Pipihkan adonan dan letakan potongan gula merah di tengah, bulatkan.
  7. Masukan bola adonan kedalam air mendidih, tunggu hingga bola bola klepon mengambang.
  8. Baluri klepon dengan kelapa parut dan hidangkan!

Ayam Panggang

Preparation time: 15 minutes
Cooking time: 45 minutes
Servings: 2 portions
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Sweet, savory, tender and juicy! All Indonesians' favorite dish: Ayam panggang kecap.
  • 1 chicken (cut into 4 pieces)
  • 1 tsp tamarind water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 bird's eye chilies
  • 8 shallots
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 ekor ayam (potong 4 bagian)
  • 1 sdt air asam jawa
  • 1 sdt garam
  • 6 sdm kecap asin
  • 1 sdt gula pasir
  • 4 cabai rawit
  • 8 bawang merah
  • 4 siung bawang putih
  • 1 sdt lada putih
  • 3 sdm mentega
  1. Marinate chicken with tamarind water and salt, put aside
  2. In a saucepan, stir fry the ground spices with butter until fragrant
  3. Put the marinated chicken into the saucepan
  4. Add 650ml of water, soy sauce, sugar and salt
  5. Put in low heat until chicken is tender and sauce almost dry
  6. Set aside the chicken and keep the sauce
  7. Preheat the oven at 180° C.
  8. Put the chicken on the tray, pour sauce on top of chicken and spread all over the surface
  9. Roast chicken until well cooked and caramelized (~15 minutes)
  10. Serve and enjoy!
  1. Marinasi ayam dengan air asam jawa dan garam
  2. Dalam panci, tumis bumbu halus dengan mentega hingga harum
  3. Masukkan ayam yang sudah dimarinasi ke dalam panci
  4. Tambahkan 650ml air, kecap asin, gula dan garam
  5. Panaskan dengan api kecil hingga ayam empuk dan saus sampai hampir kering
  6. Sisihkan ayam dan simpan sausnya
  7. Panaskan oven terlebih dahulu dengan suhu 180° C.
  8. Letakkan ayam di atas nampan, tuangkan saus di atas ayam dan oleskan ke seluruh permukaan
  9. Panggang ayam sampai matang dan berwarna coklat keemasan (~15 menit)
  10. Sajikan dan nikmati!

Tempe Orek

Preparation time: 20 minutes
Cooking time: 30 minutes
Servings: 5 pieces
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
A side dish that every Indonesian misses and loves with rice.
  • 500g tempe
  • 250g green bean
  • 2 red chili
  • 6 shallots (sliced)
  • 4 cloves of garlic (minced)
  • 1 lemongrass
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 4 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 500g tempe
  • 250g buncis
  • 2 cabe merah
  • 6 siung bawang merah (iris)
  • 4 siung bawang putih (cincang)
  • 3 daun salam
  • 1 sereh
  • 1 sdt garam
  • 1 sdt merica
  • 4 sdm kecap manis
  1. Heat up some oil in a wok and deep fry the tempe
  2. Boil in a pot/ kettle to blanch the green beans, blanch for around 5 minutes/ half cooked
  3. In another heated wok or frying pan, place some oil and saute the shallots, garlic, bay leaves, chilies, and lemongrass until fragrant.
  4. When the aromatics are fragrant, put in the blanched green beans and fried tempe
  5. Put in seasonings (salt, pepper, sweet soy sauce), taste, and adjust according to your liking.
  6. When everything is well mixed, you are ready to serve. Enjoy!
  1. Panaskan minyak di panci dan goreng tempe
  2. Rebus buncis di air mendidih untuk 5 menit atau sampai setengah matang
  3. Panaskan panci, taro sedikit minyak dan tumiskan bawang merah, bawang putih, daun salam, sereh, dan cabai hingga harum
  4. Tarokan buncis dan tempe
  5. Tarokan garam, merica dan kecap manis. (Bisa ditambah atau kurang sesuai selera)
  6. Setelah bumbu-bumbu telah diaduk rata, tempe orek siap disajikan!
