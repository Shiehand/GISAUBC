Martabak Manis

Preparation time: 1-2 hours
Cooking time: 15 minutes
Servings: 3-4 portions
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Description: Sweet, fulfilling and for sure will remind Indonesians of home. The one and only Martabak🤪
  • 250 g all purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 4 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • Condensed Milk
  • 3 handful of chocolate chunk/meses
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate soda
  • 2 handful of ground peanut
  • 75 g sugar
  • 150 g cheddar cheese
  • 350 ml water
  • 1 egg
  • 250 g tepung serbaguna
  • ¼ sdt garam
  • 4 sdm mentega
  • Susu kental manis
  • 3 genggam coklat/meses
  • ½ sdt soda bikarbonat
  • 2 genggam kacang
  • 75 g gula
  • 150 g keju cheddar
  • 350 ml air
  • 1 telur
  1. Making the batter
    1. Mix 25 g sugar and egg, then add 350 ml water and mix it well before adding flour and salt.
    2. Whisk until no more lumps and let it set for duration of 1-2 hours.
  2. Preparing
    1. While preheating the pan, mix bicarbonate soda with 1 tsp of water. Add the mixture to batter and mix well.
    2. Make sure the pan is hot and is on low heat before pouring half of the batter. Don’t forget to spread the batter to the edge of the pan to make the crust.
    3. Leave the batter until bubble starts to surface.
    4. When it is half way done, take sugar and sprinkle on top of the martabak. Put lead on the pan and wait until it is done (crispy and golden brown on the outside, moist in the inside).
    5. When it is done and still hot, spread 2 tbs of butter over the martabak, and then cut into half.
  3. Adding the toppings
    1. Add or adjust to your liking
    2. Put chocolate chunks or meses on half of the martabak, and put shredded cheddar cheese on the other half.
    3. Sprinkle crush peanuts, sesame seed and lastly, condensed milk, all over the surface.
    4. Lastly, to server, fold the halved martabak inwards, with fillings inside.
    5. Cut evenly, serve and enjoy!
  1. Adonan
    1. Campur 25g gula dan telur, lalu tambahkan 350 ml air dan aduk rata sebelum menambahkan tepung dan garam.
    2. Kocok hingga tidak menggumpal lagi dan diamkan selama 1-2 jam.
  2. Martabak
    1. Saat memanaskan panci, campurkan soda bikarbonat dengan 1 sdt air. Tambakan campuran ke adonan dan aduk rata.
    2. Tambahkan campuran ke adonan dan aduk rata. Pastikan wajan panas, menggunakan api kecil, sebelum menuangkan setengah adonan ke dalam.
    3. Diamkan adonan hingga muncul gelembung-gelembung kecil.
    4. Saat masih setengah matang, taburkan gula di atas martabak.
    5. Taruh timah di wajan dan tunggu sampai matang (renyah dan coklat keemasan di luar, lembab di dalam).
    6. Setelah matang, olesi 2 sdm mentega di atas martabak, lalu potong menjadi dua.
  3. Topping
    1. Tambahkan dan sesuaikan dengan keinginan anda.
    2. Taruh potongan coklat atau meses di atas separuh martabak, dan taburkan keju cheddar yang sudah di parut di atas separuhnya lagi.
    3. Jika mau, tambahkan kacang, wijen dan susu kental manis di seluruh permukaan martabak.
    4. Lipat martabak yang sudah dibelah dua, bagian topping di dalam.
    5. Potong rata, sajikan dan nikmati!

Tahu Cabe Garam

Preparation time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 10 minutes
Servings: 3-4 portions
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Tahu Cabe Garam, a dish that is craved by almost everyone. Your go to tofu recipe! Easy to make and yummy in your tummy. Eat it with a bowl of warm rice or as it is, it could never go wrong!
  • 6 firm tofu
  • 5 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 5 Tbsp corn starch
  • 2 Tbsp potato starch
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp mushroom bouillon
  • 10 cloves of garlic
  • 2 pcs of red chilli
  • 2 stems of green onion
  • Cooking oil
  • 6 buah tahu putih
  • 5 sdm tepung serbaguna
  • 5 sdm tepung maizena
  • 2 sdm tepung sagu
  • ½ sdt garam
  • ½ sdt kaldu jamur
  • 10 siung bawang putih
  • 2 buah cabe merah
  • 2 batang daun bawang
  • Minyak goreng
  1. In a bowl, combine mushroom bouillon, cornstarch, AP flour and potato starch.
  2. Cut chilli and green onions into smaller pieces. Mince garlic.
  3. Cut tofu into 6 small cubes.
  4. Coat tofu evenly with the flour mixture.
  5. Heat up the cooking oil, once it starts to bubble add in coated tofu. Deep fry until dry and brown. Set it aside.
  6. Heat up 3 tbsp of cooking oil. Add in chilli and green onions, stir fry until fragrant.
  7. Add in garlic and stir fry until dry and brown. Add in salt and mix!
  8. Add in fried tofu and mix well!
  9. Serve!
  1. Campurkan kaldu jamur, tepung maizena, tepung sagu dan tepung serbaguna dalam wadah.
  2. Potong cabai merah dan daun bawang hingga kecil. Cincang halus bawang putih.
  3. Potong tahu hingga menjadi 6 kotak kecil.
  4. Baluri tahu dengan campuran tepung dan kaldu jamur.
  5. Panaskan minyak (cukup untuk menggoreng tahu). Goreng tahu hingga kering dan kecoklatan. Angkat dan tiriskan.
  6. Panaskan 3 sdm minyak. Masukan cabai merah dan daun bawang hingga harum dan agak kering.
  7. Goreng bawang putih cincang dengan api kecil sampai kering dan kecoklatan. Tambahkan garam.
  8. Masukkan tahu goreng dan aduk rata.
  9. Hidangkan!

Mie Ayam

Preparation time: 30 minutes
Cooking time: 30 minutes
Servings: 2 portions
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Your typical Sunday morning breakfast, that most of us Indonesians enjoy and surely miss, mie ayam.
  • 2 portions noodles (fresh noodle preferably)
  • Aromatic Ingredients:
  • 5 gloves of garlic
  • 7 cloves of shallots
  • 2 candlenuts
  • 1 cm ginger
  • 1 cm turmeric
  • 3 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • Chicken topping ingredients:
  • 300 g chicken fillet, diced
  • Water (as needed)
  • 1 lemongrass
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 3 lime leaf
  • 1-2 Tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp mushroom bouillon
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 green onion, finely sliced
  • Savory soup ingredients:
  • 50 ml hot water/chicken stock
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp mushroom bouillon
  • Aromatic oil ingredients:
  • 30 g garlic, minced
  • 30 ml vegetable oil
  • Other toppings:
  • 10 pieces of boiled Choy Sum
  • 1 stem of green onion, sliced
  • Your favorite hot sauce!
  • 2 porsie mie
  • Bahan Bumbu Halus:
  • 5 siung bawang putih
  • 7 siung bawang merah
  • 2 kemiri
  • 1 cm jahe
  • 1 cm kunyit
  • 3 sdm minyak goreng
  • Bahan Topping Ayam:
  • 300 g paha ayam fillet, potong dadu
  • Air secukupnya
  • 1 batang serai
  • 1 lembar daun salam
  • 1-2 sdm kecap manis
  • 1 sdm saus tiram
  • ¼ sdt merica
  • ½ sdt kaldu jamur
  • ½ sdt garam
  • 1 sdt gula
  • 1 batang daun bawang
  • Bahan Air Gurih:
  • 50 ml air panas/kuah ayam
  • ½ sdt garam
  • ½ sdt kaldu jamur
  • Bahan minyak aromatic:
  • 30 g bawang putih, dicincang
  • 30 ml minyak goreng
  • Topping lain:
  • 10 lembar caisim, rebus
  • 1 batang daun bawang, iris
  • Saus sambal sesuai selera
  1. Prepare the savory stock by mixing all the ingredients together until the mushroom bouillon dissolves.
  2. For the aromatic oil, in cold oil place the minced garlic and gently heat in low flame until the garlic is golden brown then set aside.
  3. For the aromatics blend everything in a blender or food processor till it turns into a paste.
  4. Once it’s all blended into a paste, heat the oil in the pan in medium heat, and stir fry with lemongrass, bay leaf, lime leaves.
  5. When fragrant, add some water to deglaze the pan, and add the chicken together with salt, pepper, sugar, sweet soy sauce, oyster sauce, mushroom bouillon. Cook until the chicken is well done.
  6. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook your noodles until cooked (depends on the noodles you bought, read the packet instructions).
  7. To serve, place a few teaspoons of aromatic oil in the bottom of the bowl (cater to your own liking) and mix your noodles in together with the oil.
  8. Top your bowl of noodles with your chicken topping, cooked caisim, green onions and sambal according to your liking.
  9. Serve with your bowl of savory stock.
  10. Enjoy!
  1. Untuk air gurih, campurkan semua bahan-bahan sampai rata.
  2. Untuk aromatic oil, di panci dingin, taro minyak dan bawang putih cincang. Panaskan dengan api kecil sampai bawang putih kecoklatan.
  3. Setelah diblender, di panci panaskan minyak, dan tumis bumbu halus dengan serai, daun salam, dan daun jeruk sampai harum.
  4. Setelah harum, tambahkan air, ayam, garam, gula, kecap manis, saus tiram, kaldu jamur.k
  5. Sisihkan ayam topping.
  6. Bawa air sampai mendidih lalu masak mie sampai matang.
  7. Untuk sajih, taro beberapa sendok teh aromatic oil kedalam mangkok, aduk dengan bakmi.
  8. Taro topping ayam, caisim rebus, daun bawang dan sambal sesuai selera.
  9. Sajikan dengan air gurih.
  10. Nikmati!
