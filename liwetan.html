ABOUT LIWETAN

On the 23rd of October, GISAU held our annual "Liwetan" at KJRI. We were greatly surprised by the enthusiasm our participants shared in our activities.

Of course, this event couldn't be possible without the support of KJRI and our sponsors Tempea, Climbbase5, Tealeaves and Steve’s Poke . We would also like to give a special thanks to Bpk. Prakoso Wicaksono, for giving a wonderful speech to open our event.

All in all, GISAU wants to thank you for coming to LIWETAN and we hope to see you in our next event!
