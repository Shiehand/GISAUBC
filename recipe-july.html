Cireng

Preparation time: 15 minutes
Cooking time: 15 minutes
Servings: 15 pieces
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
A simple yet easy-to-miss dish! This will be THE fun activity for you and your friends to try. Making cireng is fun with friends because it is easier to make with another set of hands! The ingredients are easy to find and might probably be in your kitchen already! The crispy outside and the chewy inside will make you drool for more!
  • 20 Tbsp of starch flour
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 stems of scallions
  • 4 Tbsp of flour
  • 250 ml of water
  • 1 tsp of mushroom powder
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp of salt
  • Vegetable oil
  • 20 sdm tepung tapioka
  • 3 siung bawang putih
  • 1 daun bawang
  • 4 sdm tepung terigu
  • 250 ml air
  • 1 sdt kaldu jamur
  • 1 sdt garam
  • 1 sdt lada putih
  • Minyak goreng
  1. Wash all the produce.
  2. Cut the scallions into smaller pieces.
  3. Mince the garlic (Use garlic mincer if available)
  4. Add in 4 Tbsp of flour, 8 Tbsp of tapioca flour, scallions and garlic to a bowl and mix them thoroughly.
  5. Boil water
  6. Mix the flour, tapioca flour, and scallions while adding boiling water until a clear paste is formed.
  7. Add in 12 Tbsp of starch flour and mix well.
  8. Shape the paste into cireng shape. Tip: dust your hands with flour to prevent waste on hands.
  9. Heat up the cooking oil, once it starts to bubble, put in the cireng paste.
  10. After 3-4 minutes, flip and let it sit for another 3 minutes.
  1. Cuci semua bahan masak.
  2. Potong kecil daun bawang.
  3. Cincang halus bawang putih (bisa menggunakan alat pemotong bawang putih)
  4. Masukan 4 sdm tepung terigu dan 8 sdm tepung tapioka ke dalam mangkuk dan tambahkan potongan daun bawang dan bawang putih.
  5. Panaskan 250mL air hingga mendidih.
  6. Tambahkan air mendidih ke dalam mangkuk. Tip: Aduk dan masukan air mendidih secara perlahan untuk mendapatkan konsistensi yang baik.
  7. Tambahkan 12 sdm tepung tapioka dan aduk rata.
  8. Bentuk adonan hingga menjadi bulat yg pipih (bentuk cireng) Tip: Taburi sedikit tepung ke tangan agar tidak lengket
  9. Panaskan minyak goreng dan masukan cireng.
  10. Setelah 3-4 menit , balik dan biarkan selama 3-4 menit.
  11. Angkat dan tiriskan minyak. Hindangkan selagi panas!

Acar Timun

Preparation time: 7 minutes
Cooking time: 0 minutes
Servings: 1 medium jar
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
The best side dish to clean your palate. It’s easy to make and you can never go wrong with it. A fun fact about pickles, it helps with indigestion and fights diseases!
  • 500 g of cucumber
  • 100 grams of white sugar
  • 1 Tbsp of white vinegar
  • 1 tsp of salt
  • 500 g timun
  • 100 g gula pasir
  • 1 sdm cuka putih
  • 1 sdt garam
  1. Wash the cucumbers.
  2. Cut down the cucumbers into smaller pieces (half a circle with approximately 1cm in height).
  3. Add all of them into a small bowl (preferably a glass bowl / container with lid).
  4. Add in sugar, salt and vinegar.
  5. Mix them up with a spoon, let it rest at room temperature for around 2 hours.
  6. You should see water at the bottom of the bowl after 2 hours
  7. Cover the bowl and leave it overnight in the fridge and your pickle is good to eat the next day. Don’t forget to mix it up before the first try! The acidity will develop over the next couple of days.
  1. Cuci timun.
  2. Potong timun hingga berbentuk setengah lingkaran dengan tinggi kurang lebih 1cm.
  3. Masukan timun ke dalam mangkuk kaca.
  4. Tambahkan gula, garam dan cuka putih.
  5. Aduk hingga rata, biarkan di suhu ruang kurang lebih 2 jam
  6. Setelah 2 jam, akan terlihat air dibawah mangkuk
  7. Tutup dan masukkan ke dalam kulkas dan tinggalkan semalaman. Acar bisa langsung dimakan keesokan hari. Jangan lupa untuk mengaduk sebelum dimakan. Jika ingin lebih asam, acar bisa disimpan lebih lama.

Semur Daging Kentang

Preparation time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 60 minutes
Servings: 2-3 people
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Built around beef and swimming in fresh ginger, a warm bowl of semur daging kentang is guaranteed to shoo away your winter chills. Meanwhile, in the summer, the tenderness of beef and the soft potatoes will surely refresh your head with the taste of home. Semur daging kentang is truly the staple for every Indonesian household and a MUST for students to try.
  • 500 grams beef (chuck, round, rump)
  • 1 Tbsp butter, melted
  • 950 ml of water
  • 1 large potato, peeled and cut into large chunks
  • 1 large tomato, quartered
  • 4 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2.5 cloves peeled garlic, finely chopped
  • 0.5 inch fresh ginger
  • 1 bay leaves
  • Fried shallots for garnish (optional)
  • 2 Tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • ½ Tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ Tbsp coconut sugar or more to taste
  • Salt to taste
  • 500 gram daging sapi
  • 1 sdm mentega, dilelehkan
  • 950mL air
  • 1 kentang, dikupas dan dipotong sama besar dengan daging
  • 1 tomat, dipotong 4
  • 4 bawang merah, dicincang
  • 2.5 siung bawang putih, dicincang
  • 1.5 cm jahe
  • 1 daun salam
  • Bawang merah goreng (optional)
  • 2 sdm kecap manis
  • ½ sdm kecap asin
  • ½ sdm gula jawa
  • Garam sesuai selera
  1. Blend shallots, garlic and ginger in a food processor.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pot.
  3. Add in the mixture in the food processor and bay leaves then stir fry until fragrant.
  4. Add the beef, sweet soy sauce, soy sauce and coconut sugar into the pot.
  5. Pour in 4 cups of water and bring to a boil
  6. Lower the heat and simmer for about 45 minutes to 1 hour (or until the meat is tender).
  7. Add potatoes and tomatoes after 30 minutes of cooking.
  8. Gently stir to mix.
  9. Garnish with fried shallot.
  1. Gunakan blender (food processor) untuk menghaluskan bawang merah, bawang putih dan jahe.
  2. Lelehkan mentega di panci besar.
  3. Masukkan bumbu halus yang ada di food processor dan daun salam ke dalam pot. Aduk hingga wangi.
  4. Tambahkan daging sapi, kecap manis, kecap asin dan gula jawa ke dalam panci.
  5. Tambahkan air dan tunggu hingga mendidih.
  6. Kecilkan api dan biarkan selama 45-60 menit. Tunggu hingga daging empuk.
  7. Tambahkan kentang dan tomat setelah 30 menit.
  8. Aduk secara perlahan.
  9. Hidangkan menggunakan bawang merah goreng.
