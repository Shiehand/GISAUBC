Leaving for university overseas is so bittersweet. It is a part of growing, maturing, and moving onwards with new challenges. Yet given the sheer size of the UBC campus and its population, students are commonly found to feel overwhelmed.

Through UBUD, short for UBC Udah Dekat, GISAU aims to inform parents and students about life at UBC or in Vancouver. Expect fun educational and interactive presentations, as well as social and networking activities. To avoid any confusions, UBUD was previously known as Destination Canada. But fear not! Despite the new name, UBUD still possesses Destination Canada’s high quality presenters and event rundowns (if not better) and is still targeted to help first-year and transfer students coming to UBC.

Due to COVID-19, this year’s UBUD will be held online on July 23, from 7:00 pm - 8:35 pm (PDT) or 9:00 am - 10:35 am (WIB). RSVP here now because spots are limited!

But before letting out a big sigh and thinking, ‘no, not another online event.’  Let me tell you why UBUD is more than just another online event.

“[Destination Canada] has allowed me to [learn more] about UBC and Canada, especially about programs and ways we can make Canada a little bit more like home.”

- Janice Suhardja, 2nd year in the Bachelor of Arts

UBUD provides the opportunity to engage with other incoming students

Are you anxious about the prospect of socializing in a brand new environment? Then this event is for you! You’ll receive the chance to kickstart a meeting with other freshmen because UBUD will have fun games and interesting prompt questions.

UBUD facilitates queries from both parents and students

Ask away your concerns to an upper-year student in our break-out room sessions while your parents bring up their questions to Mr. Gadjah Mada, a well-informed parent on life at UBC. Meanwhile, students will be divided according to their faculties to have their worries answered in smaller break-out rooms.

UBUD gives students insights about the wider Indonesian community in Vancouver

When asked about the upcoming event, GISAU Events Director Nadya Evangeline highlighted that the great thing about UBUD is its cohesion, innovation, and close-knit collaboration with other communities such as PERMIKA and iSOTO. “I know this event can’t happen in person anymore,” said Nadya, “but the whole team has been brainstorming for creative ways to improve [UBUD], including asking for feedback from last year’s attendees.”
