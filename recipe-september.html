Perkedel Kentang

Preparation time: 20 minutes
Cooking time: 10 minutes
Servings: 10 pieces
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Perkedel Kentang is one of the most famous Indonesian side dishes. Fun fact, “perkedel” was derived from “frikandel”, a Dutch term for deep-fried minced meat patties. Perkedel is usually enjoyed with soup dishes, such as soto and rawon.
  • 3 Large potatoes
  • Water
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp White pepper
  • 1 tsp Mushroom bouillon
  • 2 big Eggs
  • Cooking oil
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 3 buah kentang
  • Air
  • 1 sdt Garam
  • 1 sdt Lada putih
  • 1 sdt Kaldu jamur
  • 2 telur
  • Minyak goreng
  • 3 siung bawang putih, dicincang
  1. Boil water.
  2. Cut potatoes in half and put them into boiling water.
  3. Boil potatoes for 10-12 minutes until it is soft enough to be poke with a fork.
  4. Remove potato skin and mash them with a fork or potato masher until smooth.
  5. Crack eggs, separate the yolk and the whites.
  6. Add in mushroom bouillon, white pepper, salt and garlic. Mix well.
  7. Shape into patties and dip into the egg whites.
  8. Heat up the cooking oil, once it starts to bubble, put in the shaped potatoes.
  9. Flip after 3-4 minutes, and cook for another 3 minutes.
  10. Let it sit on a wire rack to remove excess oil and serve!
  1. Rebus air hingga mendidih.
  2. Potong menjadi dua bagian dan rebus.
  3. Rebus selama 10-12 menit atau hingga kentang empuk dan bisa ditusuk menggunakan garpu.
  4. Kupas kulit kentang dan hancurkan menggunakan alat bantu sampai halus.
  5. Pecahkan telur dan pisahkan kuning dan putihnya.
  6. Tambahkan kaldu jamur, lada putih, garam dan bawang putih. Aduk rata.
  7. Bentukan kentang dan celupkan kedalam putih telur.
  8. Panaskan minyak goreng dan masukan kentang jika sudah panas.
  9. Setelah 3-4 menit, dibalik dan digoreng selama 3 menit.
  10. Angkat dan tiriskan minyak, lalu hidangkan!

Martabak Telur

Preparation time: 65 minutes
Cooking time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4 pieces
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Source: https://freepikpsd.com/martabak-telor-png-transparent-images/195131/
If you’re craving for something crispy, Martabak Telur is the way to go! It might take a while to make, but the result will be worth it! The combination of its crispy skin with the delicious filling will make you crave for more!
  • Skin:
  • 200 grams high-protein flour
  • 140 mL water
  • 30 grams cooking oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • Oil
  • Fillings:
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 stalks of large green onion
  • 300 grams of minced meat
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1/2 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 4 tbsp oil to fry
  • Bahan Kulit:
  • 200 gr tepung terigu protein tinggi
  • 140 ml air
  • 30 gr minyak goreng
  • 1/4 sdt garam
  • secukupnya minyak goreng untuk rendaman
  • Bahan Isian:
  • 4 butir telur bebek atau telur ayam
  • 3 batang daun bawang ukuran besar, iris
  • 300 gr daging sapi cincang
  • 3 siung bawang putih, cincang
  • 1/2 buah bawang bombai ukuran besar, iris memanjang
  • 1 1/4 sdt garam
  • 1 sdt lada putih
  • 2 sdt gula pasir
  • 1/4 sdt pala bubuk
  • 4 sdm minyak untuk menumis
  • secukupnya minyak untuk menggoreng
  1. (Skin) Mix flour and salt, add in water little by little while mixing. Add in oil and continue mixing.
  2. Divide the dough into 4 little pieces (around 80-85gr), soak in oil for 1 hour.
  3. (Filling) Add in 3 tbsp of oil and add in garlic and onions. Once caramelized, add in meat and stir fry until it’s cooked.
  4. Add in eggs, green onions, salt, white pepper, sugar and nutmeg. Mix well.
  5. Heat up oil in a shallow pan using low heat.
  6. Take 1 dough, place it on a surface covered with oil, press on the dough gently and place it on the pan. Add in 1/4 of the filling in, fold gently and cook with low heat.
  7. Let it sit in the cooling rack and serve!
  1. (Kulit) Campurkan terigu dan garam, tambahkan air sedikit demi sedikit sambil diuleni. tambahkan minyak, uleni kembali.
  2. Bagi adonan menjadi 4 buah (sekitar 80-85 gr), rendam dalam minyak yang banyak selama kurang lebih 1 jam, sisihkan.
  3. (Isian) Panaskan 3 sdm minyak, masukkan bawang putih dan bawang bombay, tumis sampai agak harum, masukkan daging, tumis sampai matang, sisihkan.
  4. Tambahkan telur, daun bawang, garam, lada, gula pasir dan pala bubuk, kocok sampai rata.
  5. Panaskan wajan ceper dengan minyak secukupnya dengan api kecil.
  6. Ambil 1 buah adonan, di alas yang sudah dilumuri minyak, tekan-tekan adonan sampai agak tipis (hati-hati jangan sampai sobek), angkat adonan langsung ke wajan, beri 1/4 adonan isian, lipat perlahan, masak dengan api kecil.
  7. Angkat dan tiriskan minyak, lalu hidangkan!

Orek Tempe Basah

Preparation time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 15 minutes
Servings: 1-2 people
Difficulty:
Spiciness:
Source: https://www.masakapahariini.com/resep/orek-tempe-basah/
The best definition of home away from home! Being in Vancouver, tempe might be hard to find, but if you do find one, orek tempe basah is the dish to go!
  • 300 gr tempe, diced
  • 3 cloves of garlic, miced
  • 3 cloves of red onions, minced
  • 3 Tbsp kecap manis (sweet soy sauce)
  • 200 ml water
  • Vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Oyster Sauce
  • 300 gr tempe, dipotong dadu
  • 3 siung bawang putih, dicincang
  • 3 siung bawang merah, dicincang
  • 3 sdm kecap manis
  • 200 ml air
  • Minyak sayur
  • 1 sdt garam
  • 1 sdt gula
  • 1 sdm saus tiram
  1. Deep-fry the tempe using vegetable oil, until half-cooked. Set aside.
  2. Pour in 3 tbsp of vegetable oil into a pan and heat it up.
  3. Add garlic and red onions and stir fry until fragrant.
  4. Add half-cooked tempe, kecap manis (sweet soy sauce), salt, sugar, oyster sauce and water.
  5. Lower the heat and simmer for about 5-7 minutes
  6. Serve with rice!
  1. Goreng tempe setengah matang menggunakan minyak sayur.
  2. Tambahkan 3 sdm minyak sayur kedalam panci dan panaskan.
  3. Tambahkan bawang putih dan bawang merah, tumis hingga wangi.
  4. Tambahkan tempe, kecap manis, garam, gula, saus tiram dan air.
  5. Kecilkan api dan masak selama 5-7 menit.
  6. Hidangkan dengan nasi!
