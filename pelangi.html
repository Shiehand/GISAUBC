P E L A N G I

A full week of drizzle won’t rain on our parade. PELANGI was inevitably one of the events many people looked forward to. We had tons of food, games, and opportunities to mingle around with other Indonesians.

ABOUT PELANGI

Pelangi is our welcoming event of the year, previously known as T.G.I.F, where we welcome all students back to campus. This event was held on the 24th of September, where we gathered at the UBC Nest rooftop.

We began the event with an introduction by our vice-president Chloe, followed by a trivia game emceed by Marina and Valerie. Pelangi was topped of with ayam suwir, kentang balado, and telor kecap as our dinner, which was prepared by our FnB Director, Cia.

All in all, GISAU wants to thank you for coming to PELANGI and we hope to see you in our next event!
