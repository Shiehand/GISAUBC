When you’ve been living under the comfort of your parent’s care, learning about the system may present a shock to you. It takes time to get used to monitoring your spendings and evaluating your budget. It’s also easy to feel lost because our high school never taught us about personal finances.

Some of the questions include how do we build wealth? Or how do we prevent overspending? And the answer is simple: you need to start creating a budgeting plan that works.

BELOW ARE 5 POINTERS TO CURATING A SOLID PLAN FOR YOURSELF:

BUDGET PLANNING, BANKING, AND PART-TIME JOBS

CONSIDER THE 10 ABSOLUTE PRIORITIES OF YOUR BUDGET

Once you’ve discovered the right template for yourself, the next step is to set aside a space for the 10 important elements listed below.

  1. Accommodation (rent)
  2. Food
  3. Transportation
  4. Socializing
  5. Insurance
  6. Clothes
  7. Books
  8. Utilities
  9. Mobile plans
  10. Miscellaneous

Iervinny suggests allotting at least 20-30% of your money for miscellaneous circumstances. Unexpected purchases and emergencies are inevitable no matter how small they are. Thus, it’s best to have a backup.

CHOOSE THE BEST WAY TO MONITOR YOUR SPENDINGS

People budget on their terms and you need to find what works best for you. Some prefer tracking their money the traditional way while others prefer to use apps or digital templates. For the latter, Titan Tyra’s financial tracker or Money Helper’s budgeting plan are excellent resources. Additionally, Money Lover is an excellent money app with user-friendly features and an interface.

MAKE THE MOST OUT OF YOUR BANK, STUDENT DISCOUNTS, AND PART TIME JOBS

Most small and large businesses have student discounts, so make the most out of them. In addition, you ought to understand how banks work. The first step is to choose a bank. The Student Life Network website is a great source to see the Pros and Cons of different banks.

The next step is learning how to maximize your bank account. Iervinny gave an example of opening a savings account to earn interest. If you’re interested in learning more, Investopedia has outlined how a savings account works.

You can also apply for part-time jobs or work learn positions on UBC’s Careers Online. Otherwise, you can apply for off-campus jobs through job search websites. The Robert Half website is an excellent starting point!

A REALITY CHECK

Ultimately, no one cares about your finances but yourself. Thus, you are responsible for the actions you take. Simple planning and research can go a long way.

LAST WORDS

Don’t beat yourself up if you spend more money than planned because handling money can be tricky. Iervinny highlighted how it took her a while to adjust to a strict budget and plan out her income. So if you’re struggling to keep your feet on the ground, give yourself some time to adjust. It’s okay to make mistakes because that’s how we become better!
